Enhanced Diagnosis Engine

Hybrid AI Fusion

Our engine implements a weighted consensus algorithm. It fuses high-precision visual recognition from OpenRouter AI Vision (Qwen 2.5-VL, 70% weight) with deep-layer validation against our local East African expert knowledge base (30% weight).

Guided Visual Analysis

Unlike generic AI, our system uses pre-submission context. It intelligently briefs the vision models on crop-specific symptoms prevalent in Kenya before analysis, drastically reducing false positives.

Local Contextualization

Treatment plans are now dynamically mapped to KALRO and FAO East Africa guidelines, ensuring that recommended products and practices are accessible and effective for Kenyan smallholders.

Note: While our confidence scores are high, results should be verified by a local agricultural extension officer before applying intensive chemical treatments.
